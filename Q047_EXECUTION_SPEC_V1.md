   invariant rectangles and strict tube/domain checks.
3. A right neighborhood cannot shorten the proof interval. Reject negative
   widths, partition gaps, overlaps, discontinuities and invalid source domains.

6. Changed package/source identity, unknown ID, missing outputs, contradictory
